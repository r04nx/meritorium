STUDENTS REVIEW
My name is Snehal. While pursuing M.B.A I joined the two months Data Science course conducted by MCED in Nagpur. It was a day long rigorous training in which we had came across many in-demand tools like Anaconda, statistical data analysis using python, few basic concepts of machine learning etc. After successful completion of this course, during a placement drive in college I got selected as a Research Analyst in the field of financial analytics. The exposure to python and machine learning actually made a strong base of mine and helped me gain more confidence as my skillset got updated. I'm thankful to all the delegates who were involved in making this course successful.

Hi, I am Swapnali Janrao. I have done Master's in Electrical Engineering. Recently, I have completed a 2 months training in Applied Artificial Intelligence, emphasis on Internet of Things and Robotics at Pune, conducted by Meritorium Knowledge Academy in partnership with MCED.This course consisted of training in foundational concepts of statistics, probability theory, Linear Algebra, Exploratory Data Analytics, Logistic regression, Unsupervised learning, etc. All these concepts and algorithms were taught using Python. The second part of the training was in various concepts of IOT. These Iot concepts were taught to us using arduino.The training also included regular lab sessions and hands on projects which gave us an opportunity to do practicals of all the theory conducted in the classes.We also learned the various practical implementation of IoT in real life such as Home automation system, Attendance system, IoT architecture, I2c communication, Building Google Assistance. This knowledge will help me for my career in future. The training enhance my knowledge and skills and I got to learn new programming language like Python. I thank Mr. Jayant Ramteke Sir, the Director of Meritorium Knowledge Academy for providing me such a valuable training opportunity. Thank you.
