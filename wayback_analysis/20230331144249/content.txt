WELCOME TO THE MERITORIUM KNOWLEDGE ACADEMY
Meritorium Knowledge Academy, based in Mumbai, is a training institution imparting training in the cutting edge Technology areas of
Data Science, Python Programming, Statistics and Probability, Business Analytics, Marketing Analytics, financial analytics, Artificial Intelligence, machine learning, Deep Learning, Data Visualization-PowerBI and Tableau, Robotics, Internet of Things.
Meritorium Knowledge Academy
started with a vision to deliver quality education by industry experts and global changemakers. It’s mission is to make Students & Professionals equip with skills and knowledge that industry requires.
Courses designed by Meritorium Knowledge Academy are relevant to the industry and can be understood by all those who aspire to learn. MKA helps in upgrading your professional career, developing Data Science opportunities for entrepreneurs, and upscaling your life for the future.
Meritorium Academy had partnered on multiple projects with the
Government of Maharashtra
to impart training in the area of Data Science & Analytics, IOT& Robotics to the Professionally qualified unemployed youth in Maharashtra and other states of the Country.
Meritorium Knowledge Academy
is created by alumni of the following esteemed institution
IIT Bombay

The training will make them industry ready for taking assignments in the fast-growing Data Analytics industry or start their independent entrepreneurial ventures in this field, after they complete the training.
